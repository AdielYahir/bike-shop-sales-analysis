# Bike Shop Sales Analysis

Business analysis of Bike Shop sales using Google Sheets and Looker Studio.

## Objective

Analyze sales performance and identify key business insights through data integration, KPI development, and data visualization.

## Tools

- Google Sheets
- Looker Studio

## Analysis

- Data cleaning and integration of 9 related tables.
- KPI development.
- Sales analysis by brand, category, store, product, and salesperson.
- Monthly sales and units analysis.
- Interactive dashboard development.

## Key Findings

- Trek generated approximately 60% of total revenue.
- Baldwin Bikes was the highest-revenue store.
- Mountain Bikes was the highest-revenue category.
- April recorded the highest monthly sales.

## Dashboard

Interactive sales dashboard developed in Looker Studio.
